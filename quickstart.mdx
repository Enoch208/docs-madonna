You can understand Spoke before connecting a wallet. This walkthrough uses public pages from the deployed application.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore an offer">
    Open [Spoke Explore](https://usespoke.xyz/explore). Look for the **Live testnet** label. Open a campaign and read its product price, reward rate, maximum paid levels, and settlement hold.

    A campaign's reward is paid from its merchant's funded budget. The amount shown is a policy calculation, not a guarantee that a sale will happen.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the reward path">
    Open the campaign's network view. Each node represents a sharing link with a fixed parent. A branch can grow without every ancestor being paid: the policy limits the number of eligible levels.

    The [recorded demonstration network](https://usespoke.xyz/c/3/network) contains a settled sale and a refunded sale. Select a sale to inspect its path.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a settled receipt">
    Open [sale 4](https://usespoke.xyz/receipts/10143/4). Compare the buyer payment, merchant-funded reward, paid path, settlement timeline, and transaction proof.

    This is a scripted testnet demonstration. The receipt proves contract behavior, not commercial activity.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what you want to do">
    [Merchants](/guides/merchants) create and fund offers. [Distributors](/guides/distributors) create links and claim eligible rewards. [Buyers](/guides/buyers) purchase and access their delivery.

    Use **Create account** or **Log in** when you are ready for an action that requires an account. Review each wallet request before signing.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Want to explore without transactions?

Open [Demo mode](https://usespoke.xyz/demo). It uses sample data to explain the interface and preview additional workflows. Demo balances and activity do not represent onchain funds. Read [live and demo environments](/guides/live-and-demo) before switching between them.

## Before your first testnet transaction

- Use **Monad Testnet**, chain ID **10143**, for the current settlement flow.
- SDUSD is the test token used for prices and rewards; MON pays network gas.
- Testnet gas assistance may be available from the application, subject to its limits.
- A wallet confirmation is only the beginning. Wait for transaction confirmation and indexing before treating the action as complete.
- Mainnet USDC obtained through a future funding activation will not buy testnet products.
