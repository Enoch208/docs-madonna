Spoke supports an account experience around wallet-backed actions. Public discovery and receipt pages can be read without signing in. Creating links, purchasing, claiming, and account-specific views require an authenticated account and the relevant wallet authorization.

## Sign in

Use **Create account** or **Log in**. The frontend supports passkey onboarding and external wallet connections. An external wallet signs an account challenge; passkey flows use the integrated account wallet experience. Review the requested action before approving it.

A session authenticates API access. It does not give the API permission to transfer your tokens. Contract actions still require the wallet flow.

## Earn and Sell workspaces

The workspace switcher separates distributor and merchant tasks. On desktop, the sidebar groups related pages and lets you collapse groups or switch to a compact icon rail. On smaller screens, open the menu to navigate.

- **Discover:** browse offers and people.
- **Earn:** start your distributor journey and find purchases.
- **Distribution:** manage links and inspect the network.
- **Earnings and record:** follow rewards, payouts, and reputation.
- **Sell:** access the merchant's product, campaign, sales, and budget workflows.

The interface marks sample-only destinations with **Demo**.

## Manage your account

Settings includes profile and privacy preferences, connected wallet records, passkeys, notifications, recovery checks, and session management. Use recovery guidance before depending on a new sign-in method. Revoking a session ends that session's API access; it does not reverse completed transactions.

Some multi-device onboarding and intent-persistence improvements remain on the [roadmap](/project/roadmap). A supported settings screen is not a guarantee that every future onboarding step is already synchronized across devices.

## Privacy boundaries

Public graph and reputation views respect supported presentation preferences. Public blockchain transactions remain inspectable on the network. Deleting an application account cannot erase immutable chain history or undo settlement.

Aurora mainnet funding, when enabled, requires a supported external wallet session. The current passkey transaction flow is scoped to the testnet experience.
