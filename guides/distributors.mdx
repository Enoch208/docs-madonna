title: "For distributors"
description: "Find a campaign, create a sharing link, understand your path, and claim settled rewards."
---

A distributor helps a merchant reach buyers. Your link records attribution, and a qualifying sale can pay you according to the campaign's policy. Creating a link by itself does not earn a reward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an opportunity">
    Open **Explore** or **Opportunities**. Read the product, merchant, price, reward rate, budget, paid levels, and hold. Prefer products you can explain accurately to your audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your link preview">
    Choose **Create my link**. If you arrived through another distributor's link, inspect the parent and expected path. The preview explains the split and any short-path adjustment.

    Your node's parent is fixed at creation. Review it before confirming the wallet action.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and share">
    Sign in and complete the create-link transaction. Wait for indexing, then copy your link from **Links**. Share that link when you want purchases attributed to your node.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow attribution">
    Open **Network** to inspect branches and sale paths. A person who creates a link from yours can extend the graph, but only the bounded eligible ancestry receives a sale's reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and claim">
    **Earnings** separates rewards still at risk, ready to finalize, claimable, claimed, refunded, and reversed. Use **Payouts** for the claim flow once rewards are claimable. Review and confirm the wallet transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What affects your reward?

The product price, reward rate, caps, path eligibility, level weights, short-path rule, and sale outcome all matter. A headline percentage describes the total policy reward, not necessarily your individual share.

If a buyer refunds during the hold, the reserved reward is released. If the sale finalizes, your allocated share becomes claimable. A later policy change does not rewrite a sale's accepted allocation.

## Your public record

Reputation and receipts let people inspect settled activity. Adjust visibility in your account settings to control public presentation. Hiding your profile does not hide blockchain history or remove a payout obligation.

Sample invitations and communities can be explored in Demo mode, but should not be mistaken for live distribution agreements. See [current status](/project/status).
