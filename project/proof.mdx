## Frontend-to-backend journey

It covers campaign 3, a settled sale, a refunded sale, reward claims, authenticated delivery, and refund-driven delivery revocation.

- [Campaign 3](https://usespoke.xyz/c/3) shows the public testnet offer.
- [Its network](https://usespoke.xyz/c/3/network) exposes attribution and sale outcomes.
- [Sale 4](https://usespoke.xyz/receipts/10143/4) is finalized with claimed allocations.

## Contract settlement fixture

The [Monad Testnet proof artifact](https://github.com/Enoch208/spoke/blob/main/packages/contracts/deployments/10143-proof.json) records campaigns 1 and 2, finalized sales 1 and 2, and refunded sale 3. Its **58 confirmed transactions** include gas funding.

The fixture exercises a four-level path, a branch, multiple merchants, exact allocations, refund timing, finalization, claims, and unused-budget withdrawals while liabilities remain protected. Recorded reward and buyer-payment conservation checks reconcile to the contract balance.

## Source verification

The [deployment manifest](https://github.com/Enoch208/spoke/blob/main/packages/contracts/deployments/10143.json) identifies network, addresses, and verification evidence. Matching deployed bytecode to published source improves inspectability; it does not demonstrate the absence of vulnerabilities.

## Reproduce the result

Follow [local setup](/developers/local-development), then the repository's [settlement fixture instructions](https://github.com/Enoch208/spoke/blob/main/packages/contracts/README.md). Use fresh test wallets and keep signing material private. Never present a replayed fixture as independent user activity.
